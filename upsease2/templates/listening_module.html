Test Format Overview

Last updated 20 August 2026

The listening section has four parts, each played only once, increasing in difficulty and formality as the test progresses.

The four parts

  • Part 1 — a conversation in an everyday social context
  • Part 2 — a monologue in an everyday social context
  • Part 3 — a conversation in an academic or training context
  • Part 4 — a monologue on an academic subject

General advice

Read the questions before each recording starts so you know what information to listen for. Answers often appear in the same order as the questions, and paraphrasing is common — the recording rarely uses the exact wording from the question.
