Introduction to the Test Format

Last updated 20 August 2026

This note gives an overview of the test structure and what to expect in each section.

Sections

  • Listening — four recorded parts, increasing in difficulty
  • Reading — three passages with a mix of question types
  • Writing — two tasks, a short report/letter and a longer essay
  • Speaking — a short interview, a cue card task, and a discussion

General advice

Manage your time carefully in each section. Read instructions fully before attempting a question, and review your answers if time allows.
