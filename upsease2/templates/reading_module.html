Question Types Overview

Last updated 20 August 2026

The reading section uses a range of question types across three passages. Knowing what each type expects of you before you start makes a noticeable difference to your speed and accuracy.

Common question types

  • Multiple choice — select one or more correct options
  • True / False / Not Given — judge a statement against the passage
  • Matching headings — pair paragraphs with the correct summary heading
  • Sentence completion — fill gaps using words taken from the passage
  • Short answer — respond within a strict word limit

General approach

Read the questions before the passage so you know what to look for. Underline keywords in the question, then scan the passage for matching or related terms rather than reading every line in full.
