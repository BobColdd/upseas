Test Format Overview

Last updated 20 August 2026

The speaking test has three parts, each assessing you slightly differently. Knowing what's expected in each part helps you pace your answers appropriately.

The three parts

  • Part 1 — short questions about familiar topics such as home, work, or study
  • Part 2 — a cue card task with one minute to prepare and up to two minutes to speak
  • Part 3 — a two-way discussion exploring the Part 2 topic in more depth

General advice

Aim for natural, extended answers rather than short responses. In Part 2, use your preparation time to jot down a few key points rather than writing full sentences, so you speak rather than read.
